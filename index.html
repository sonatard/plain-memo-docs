Plain Memo

A simple plain-text note app for macOS and Android.

What it does

  • Write notes as plain text. No formatting, no Markdown.
  • Sync your notes across all of your Macs and Android devices.
  • Your notes are stored on your devices and in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. There is no Plain Memo server.
  • Add several Google accounts to keep work and personal notes apart.

Google sign-in

Plain Memo asks Google for these permissions only:

  • openid and email: to tell your Google accounts apart.
  • drive.appdata: to read and write Plain Memo's own hidden folder in your Google Drive. Plain Memo cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Details are in the privacy policy.

Plain Memo について

macOS と Android で使う、ただのテキストを書くシンプルなメモアプリです。メモは端末と、あなた自身の Google Drive のアプリ専用の隠しフォルダにだけ保存し、複数の端末の間で同期します。Plain Memo のサーバーはありません。詳しくはプライバシーポリシーをご覧ください。
